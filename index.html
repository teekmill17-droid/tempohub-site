FAQ

Questions people ask.

Which executors does it work on?

We've tested on Real, Potassium, Solara, Xeno, Madium, Volt, Wave and a few others. The full list is in the Works on section. The shooting scripts work with keyboard, controller and mobile. If yours throws an error, post it in the Discord support channel and we'll look at it.

Can other players see the cosmetics?

No. Mascots, outfits, animations, meters and effects are only on your screen. Other players see your normal look, and nothing gets saved to your account. Buy it for the gameplay and treat the cosmetics as a bonus.

Can I get banned?

Any script carries some risk, and we won't pretend otherwise. Tempo presses the same buttons you would and sends what the game itself sends. It never fakes server data. Play like a normal player and don't flex features in front of mods.

What's free and what's premium?

Twelve games are completely free, Xtreme Basketball auto green included, plus a free RH2 build with tap auto green. Premium gets you RH2 hold mode, per-shot timing and cosmetics, all of Anime Battle Arena and all of Project Slayers 2.

Can I try premium first?

Yes. Chat in the Discord until you hit level 5 and a one-day key gets DMed to you. The day starts the first time you run it.

What happens when a game updates?

Check the status board in the Discord. Most updates don't touch what we time. When one does, we push a fix and post a changelog.

Can I get a refund?

If your key never worked and we can't get it running for you within 72 hours, yes. The full policy is on the refunds page.
