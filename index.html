Inside RH2

Measured, not guessed.

A layup and a floater are fifteen points apart — one target can't serve both. Each was measured in game, one point at a time, against the game's own PERFECT / EARLY / LATE verdict.

Where Tempo lets go, read off ActionValues.Power — the same value the game draws its meter from. Each is the release point, not the window.

Dunks are Space, not E. That's why most scripts don't green them — they let go of a key the game isn't watching. Tempo releases the right one.
