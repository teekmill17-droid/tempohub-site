FAQ

Straight answers.

Which executors does it work on?

It's built and tested on PC executors, and the shooting scripts support controller and mobile too. If yours has trouble, post in the Discord support channel with the error. We'd rather fix it than have you guess.

Can other players see the cosmetics?

No, and we'd rather say that here than have you find out after. Mascots, outfits, animations, meters and effects are client-side: you see them, other players see your real look, and nothing is saved to your account. Buy it for the gameplay; the cosmetics are a bonus.

Is it safe to use?

No script is risk-free, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. Tempo works through the game's own inputs and the calls the game itself makes. It doesn't fake server data. Play like a person, and don't spam features in front of mods.

What's the difference between free and premium?

Free covers thirteen games, including tap auto green in RH2 and full auto green in Xtreme Basketball. Premium unlocks RH2's hold mode, per-shot timing and cosmetics, all of Anime Battle Arena, and all of Project Slayers 2.

Can I try premium first?

Yes. Reach level 5 in our Discord by chatting and you'll get a free one-day premium key in your DMs. The day starts when you first run it.

What happens when a game updates?

The Discord status board shows what's working. Most updates don't touch what we measure, and when one does, the fix ships with a changelog.
