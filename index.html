FAQ

Which executors does it work on?

We've tested on Real, Potassium, Madium, Volt, Wave and a few others - the full list is in Supported executors above. Xeno and Solara are not supported: they're missing functions the scripts need. The shooting scripts work with keyboard, controller and mobile. If yours throws an error, post it in the Discord support channel and we'll look at it.

Can other players see the cosmetics?

No. Mascots, outfits, animations, meters and effects are only on your screen. Other players see your normal look, and nothing gets saved to your account.

Can I get banned?

There's always some risk with scripts. Tempo presses the same buttons you would and sends what the game itself sends. Play normally and don't show features off in front of mods.

What's free and what's premium?

Seventeen games are completely free, including Clover Time, Rivals, Azure Latch and auto green for RH2, Xtreme Basketball and Streetball. Premium is Clover Retribution, Anime Battle Arena, Bloodlines, Project Slayers 2, Bridger Western and Driven Football, and any premium game we add later. Weekly is $3.59, monthly $9.99, lifetime $21.99.

Can I try premium first?

Yes. Chat in the Discord until you hit level 5 and a one-day key gets DMed to you. The day starts the first time you run it.

What happens when a game updates?

Check the status board in the Discord. Most updates don't touch what we time. When one does, we push a fix and post a changelog.

Can I get a refund?

If your key never worked and we can't get it running for you within 72 hours, yes. The full policy is on the refunds page.
