Privacy

Last updated September 27, 2026

We keep as little as we can, and only what we need to deliver your key and support you.

What we collect

  • When you buy: your email, and the Discord username you enter at checkout so we can give you the Premium role. Card details go straight to our payment processor (Stripe), and we never see them.
  • When you run a key: the key and a hardware ID from our key provider (Luarmor). That's what locks a key to your device, and it lets us spot shared keys.
  • In the Discord: whatever you post there, under Discord's own privacy policy.

What we use it for

Delivering your key, giving you your role, helping you in support, and stopping key sharing and fraud. That's it. We don't sell your data, and we don't send marketing emails.

Who else sees it

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Luarmor manages keys.
  • Discord hosts the community and delivers roles.

This website

No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies. The page loads fonts from Google and asks Discord for the member count.

Deleting your data

Open a ticket in our Discord and we'll remove what we hold about you. Payment records we're required to keep for tax reasons stay with Stripe.
